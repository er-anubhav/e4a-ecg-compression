Compression ratio

The best of the two tested modes reduced the 3,000-byte raw representation to 1,717 bytes.

57.2% of the raw size remains  •  42.8% reduction  •  717 B above the 1,000 B target

What this tells us

  • The codec is lossless on this sample: every one of the 1,000 reconstructed samples matched exactly.
  • Delta-of-delta performs better than first-order delta on this sample.
  • The current two codecs do not satisfy the 1,000-byte-per-lead target.
  • This bundled dataset is synthetic, so this result is a codec benchmark, not evidence of performance on real ADS1292R recordings.
  • The next engineering step should be testing real ADS1292R 24-bit captures and evaluating stronger entropy/bit-packing schemes before firmware implementation.
